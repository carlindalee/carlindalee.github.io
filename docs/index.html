Lifecycle marketing

43–75%cut in email build time across both brands, using reusable HTML modules and AI agents I built with Claude Code

Summit thank-you email

Drove 33% of $23K+ in directly attributable revenue for a 319-attendee flagship event, across 24 email and SMS touchpoints. This send achieved a 73.33% open rate and a 31.11% click-through rate.

Made it personal with social proof: real LinkedIn posts from attendees, which built community and encouraged more guests to share. Kept the momentum going with the next event and the CEO’s LinkedIn, so readers can connect with her directly.

Personal SMS from the CEO

Averaged a 20.6% click rate across 53 sends, peaking at 34.7%.

Segmented by lifecycle stage, attendance history and user activity. Every text is written to sound personally sent by a real human, and I created behavior-triggered follow-ups to keep outreach authentic.

Welcome email with a free resource

Sent to 15.8K+ new subscribers, achieving a 56.6% open rate and a 9.5% click rate.

Led with value before any ask, to spark reciprocity. Named teammates like Bifei and added partner logos for a human, friendly tone, then closed with a no-pressure invite to get started and an open door for questions.

Personalized check-in email with externship recommendations

Sent to 8.9K+ subscribers, achieving a 47.7% open rate, a 3.8% click rate and 50+ replies.

A follow-up to the welcome email. I tagged every lead by career interest based on the blog they came in from, then matched three product offerings to each interest across 10+ versions. When readers replied, we answered their questions one-on-one.

Biweekly ‘The Edge’ newsletter

Grew the open rate from 28% to 52% year over year for a 259K+ global audience, through A/B tests and deliverability improvements.

Rewrote it in a first-person voice that gives value instead of always selling. From competitor research on Andrew Yeung’s newsletter, each issue opens with a growing subscriber count as social proof, followed by data-backed technical projects and an interactive fortune cookie. I ask readers to drag it to their main inbox and write back, then reply myself, shifting placement from Promotions to Primary.
